# The Spare Room v3.7.0 — Christmas Begins (W1)
## The Overhaul Update
*Snow has arrived.*

### Vision and evidence

The first bus should look like the safest place in Ashfield. The second time players see it, one detail should make them doubt that. Preserve exploration, interpretive endings, and persistent environmental clues. No chase loop, compulsory scare, or new progression currency.

The November Studio preview reported 66 snowy surfaces and 34 dressed buildings. The current preparation branch already records rain, puddles, a flowing fountain, a shared day clock, weather-linked sanity, three seasonal endings, and owner weather, fog and time controls. These are the starting point. Coverage counts do not measure where players look. A fresh Studio walk-through and a side-by-side comparison with the original Last Bus thumbnail remain necessary. Comparisons to high-quality Roblox horror here use design criteria: legible silhouettes, restraint, sound placement and a clear first objective.

### Lobby, detail and lighting

At spawn compose the main bus left of centre at a slight angle, amber shelter just behind its door, cold street vanishing to the right. Put alternative buses beyond the first shelter sightline, with equally legible pad numbers up close. Compare matched camera crops using the thumbnail at thumbnail size first, then test the actual default avatar camera at phone width. Preserve the walking path from spawn to bus and visibility of queue counts and countdown. Hide owner-only preview controls from ordinary accounts.

Dress the bus approach with salt bands, tyre-darkened asphalt, a shallow gutter and two puddles along the lamp path. Under the shelter add a faded fare card, damp bench edge and a timetable with one unexplained blank row. Behind the shelter use two silhouette layers of terrace roofs and trees, then mist. At the market put folded tarps, empty crates and a quiet shop window on the route. Put a missing bulb and a snow-free handprint in locations players naturally pause. Keep ambient staging out of navigable voids and avoid clues that look collectible without offering an interaction.

Review lights in rain, snow and clear weather at dusk and midnight. Use steady amber bulbs on the main shelter, muted evergreen trim and subdued red ribbons. Reuse low-emission roofline geometry with no shadows. Use a few actual local lights at the shelter and market entrances, reflection cards for the rest, and pale snow underneath. Keep dark recesses behind the tree line. Give the owner preview the decorated market, lit firs, topiary and wrapped parcels. Public winter gets snow, quiet warm windows and salt lines. Remove props that hide shelter entrances, prompts or the Watcher's silhouette.

### Weather, water and snow

Use existing weather profiles and a shared transition clock. Separate particle rate, sound, visibility, sanity modifier and anomaly weight. Fade all over roughly five seconds. Clear weather is the baseline. Rain gains exterior-only light-catching streaks, sparse road impacts and a two-stage sheltered audio filter. Snow has a slow fall and wind-aligned gusts. Storm lightning uses rare distant pulses with delayed directional thunder, capped brightness and a reduced-flash option. Fog uses a single Roblox Atmosphere for depth, with a few low-opacity local wisps in sheltered corners. Never obscure the next route marker.

Keep shelter relief and cap the combined weather/night sanity multiplier. Prototype 1.05 exposed light snow and 1.15 exposed squall before the existing Christmas drain multiplier. Increase the weight of visibility-dependent anomalies in snow and fog, with a global cooldown and individual cooldowns. No weather locks ordinary routes or forces a loss. Replicate the profile, transition time and optional seed. Clients render cosmetic effects and the server owns sanity, anomaly readiness and event eligibility.

Build wet roads from authored dark asphalt and small glossy patches at drains and curb depressions. Use a bounded pool of ripple rings and splash particles, spawn only near players in rain, and let puddles fade slowly after rain. A drain grate, downhill stains and runoff sound sell drainage without physics. Keep the existing fountain separate. Future flooding can use one authored rising water volume in an alternate run with a safe high route. Leave that experiment out of W1.

Snow covers roof ridges, bus shelter tops, planter edges, bushes, and shaded footways. Keep a dirty clear band along tyre paths and safe boarding edges. Increase drift size at leeward corners through three authored stages. Keep snow overlays collision-free and exclude them from shelter and interaction raycasts. Budget-screen cover should vary by material and zone, not by copying bright plates over every surface.

Low tier uses static caps, one local snowfall emitter and no tracks. High tier adds a capped local pool of tracks, drifts and directional flakes. Lay cosmetic prints only on tagged snow after a minimum step distance, fade over 20–40 seconds, cap at 24 near the observer, and give Watcher tracks a deliberate discontinuity. Keep deformation and replication of every footprint out of scope.

### Anomaly roadmap

Rarity below means selection weight among eligible candidates, not a guaranteed event rate. Start with common 5, uncommon 2, rare 0.6. Require safe location, no modal and no overlapping event. Sanity costs are proposed single-event percentage points, capped per encounter.

| Anomaly | Trigger | Behaviour and visual | Audio | Rarity / sanity |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Spare Key | Revisit an explored hall | Familiar key hangs on the wrong hook until player turns away | One metallic tick | Common / 0 |
| Wrong Window | Walk through a lit room at dusk | Reflection briefly shows the neighbouring room’s empty chair | Distant floorboard | Uncommon / 1 |
| Last Bus Idle | Wait by the empty depot | Bus breathes out condensation despite being off | Three-second low idle | Common / 0 |
| Snow Under Glass | Enter a sheltered market stall in snowfall | Flakes hang inside a closed display | Muted glass chime | Uncommon / 1 |
| Wind Against the Flag | Gust while outdoors near market | One pennant moves opposite the other flags | Local cloth snap | Common / 0 |
| Passenger at the Lamp | Night, exterior, unobstructed approach | A slim silhouette on the far curb disappears after occlusion | Ambient street bed ducks slightly | Uncommon / 1 |
| Wrong Chimney | Snow, near the house | Soot prints terminate at an unlit chimney | Three soft knocks and delayed bell | Uncommon / 1 |
| Fifth Parcel | At least one clue, second visit to market | A wrapped box gains the player’s name only on inspection | Paper brushing wood | Uncommon / 0 |
| Same Street Twice | Low sanity, revisit a known street marker | Number plate duplicates until the next turn. Map and collision stay unchanged | Off-axis footsteps | Rare / 2 |
| Empty Seat | One prior Watcher sighting and later snow squall | Track ends at bus door, then reflected silhouette appears during a later visit | Upholstery creak on revisit | Rare / 2 total |

First delivery: Passenger at the Lamp, Fifth Parcel and Snow Under Glass. Add later stages after telemetry verifies players see and understand the early cues. Never require a frame-perfect look. Provide a quiet journal trace once a valid observation completes.

### Watcher behaviour

Author anchors 25–65 studs from walkable viewpoints at lamps, a snowbank and the far end of a terrace. Select valid anchors using a camera-facing cone, raycast and route distance. Place outside the obvious centre of the screen, then permit a short 2–5 second glimpse. Fade behind occlusion or during snowfall, with no teleport directly in view. It may appear before the player notices, and may leave without confirmation. Evade approach by ending the encounter, without attacking or steering player movement. Mark one valid sighting per encounter and use cooldowns across anchors. Calibrate with playtesters at low visual quality. If most miss every sighting or consistently identify it at spawn, change anchors before increasing frequency.

### Owner control centre

Keep Kohl’s Admin for operational moderation when installed and configured. Use the custom panel for game-specific controls. Restrict both according to purpose and independently verify ownership and official installation before enabling Kohl’s. The prestige class keeps its existing self-only scope.

- **Overview** shows read-only place, run ID, event gate, active weather, next transition, and active anomaly/target. No global broadcast button.
- **Environment** offers Auto plus weather presets, bounded snowfall rate, timed storm and thunder tests, fog slider, dawn/noon/dusk/midnight, freeze and automatic reset. Show actual applied values.
- **Anomalies** filters by house, Christmas, weather and sanity. Trigger uses normal prerequisites. Force bypasses pacing only. Disable suppresses future natural selection for this server. Show target, active timer and clear action.
- **Player** has the current roster, target summary and a bounded sanity control. Revalidate target server-side before each mutation.
- **Endings and badges** shows missing flags, dry-run status and a preview ending screen. Testing writes no player data by default. Real award tests use provisioned test badge IDs and the owner's account.
- **Events** previews or stops permitted event scenarios inside an owner session. Changes are session-scoped. Return to Auto and restore normal state on expiry or server restart.

Every command stays server validated, rate limited and isolated to the current run. Record command, scope and outcome to a bounded server diagnostic buffer. Close state clears target selection. The current Run-tab weather, fog and time controls are the base, not a replacement target.

Measure device-specific frame times and effects counts in the same lobby and run route before and after. Target 30 FPS or better on the low-tier test device. Budget one local emitter pool per precipitation type, no full-time dynamic shadow lights for bulbs, and one-time batched static snow creation. Report p95 frame time, peak particles, instance count and join time. Stress test four-player boarding, weather switches, lowest graphics, high graphics, controller, touch and repeated return trips. Treat any camera/teleport stall or missing interaction prompt as P0 regardless of frame rate.

Five things players notice first: the bus framed through amber light, split snow and asphalt at the shelter, wind-blown snowfall outdoors, a quieter feel under cover, and an out-of-place silhouette or timetable detail.

Five independent one-day candidates using existing geometry and systems: move the spawn facing and tune focal light, add caps to the shelter and nearby roofs, restyle one common interaction prompt, author and test three Watcher viewpoints, and dress the market entry with tarps and parcels. Estimates assume one developer-day per item with a short smoke test and existing reusable assets.

Cut for W1: chase mechanics, a new economy, full-map flood, new bottom-of-screen difficulty cards, and nonlinear scope expansion across every building. Poor return on development time: real-time planar reflections per puddle, per-flake collision, networked footprints for all players, deformable snow, individual shadow-casting Christmas bulbs, bespoke animation on distant scenery.
